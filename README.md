<p>I'm a <strong>software engineer</strong> who works full stack and full cycle, from architecture and back-end to front-end and cloud infrastructure, with attention to scalable systems and clear engineering standards.</p>

<p>I started programming in 2015, at 14, studying on my own, and then took the SENAI technical course. I completed Computer Engineering at UFES in 2023 and a <strong>Master's in Electrical Engineering</strong> in 2026, with a dissertation on AI and sensor fusion with FBG sensors and four published papers in machine learning and optimization. Along the way I grew into a technical leadership role, and today I also teach mathematics, programming and music on occasion, and run my own technology company, <strong>Loomis</strong>.</p>

<p>The full story, publications and projects are in my <a href="https://pachecowillians.github.io/">portfolio</a>.</p>
